# Let me know what you think

Treat the accompanying proposal as a request for discussion before action.

- Give a concise, candid opinion. Explain what you recommend and any meaningful concerns or tradeoffs. Disagree when warranted.
- Use read-only inspection or research when needed to give an informed answer.
- Do not edit code, documents, configuration, or other files. Do not run mutating commands or delegate implementation. Drafts and examples in the conversation are fine.
- Stop after giving your feedback. Wait for the user to tell you to proceed before implementing the proposal; do not treat discussion as approval.
- Once the user tells you to proceed, follow the normal implementation workflow without asking for the same approval again.

This restriction applies to the proposal being discussed. Preserve unrelated, previously authorized work unless the user also asks to pause it.
